- **r1/Q-2** (settled against `9c70182`): What does the Markdown check flag, and which list styles may the prompt allow? → (b) Bold, italics and headings. Everything in (a), plus a single-asterisk pair `*…*` within one line whose opening `*` has no space after it and whose closing `*` has no space before it, so a `* ` bullet never matches.   - edges: What is never flagged, echoes, where the matcher lives, and whether the list is complete:

The prompt says: no asterisks or underscores for emphasis, no `#` headings, and options one per line, starting with `- ` or a number. Single-asterisk italics get through. Scope: within the issue
  - not chosen: (c) Any Markdown. Everything in (b), plus links `[text](url)`, inline code in backticks, table rows starting with a pipe, `>` quotes and `---` rules, and the prompt names each. Scope: within the issue
- **r1/Q-3** (settled against `9c70182`): What counts as "the handoff line appears twice in an escalation turn"? → (a) A phrase counted per escalation turn. The check fails when the turn's assistant texts together contain "connect you with our front desk" or "connect you with the front desk" (case-insensitive) more than once, or the clinic phone number (`CLINIC.phone`) more than once. It fails all four recorded doubles and the issue's `dev` example. It misses a pre-tool paraphrase without that phrase ("Let me get you to a person"). Scope: within the issue (accept all, [answer](https://github.com/nick-delgado/serverless-ai-scheduling/issues/233#issuecomment-6109237206))
  - edges: What an escalation turn is, which turns count, the boundary count, and the evidence:
  - not chosen: (b) Nothing about the handoff before the call. In an escalation turn, any assistant text written before the `escalate_to_human` call that mentions the front desk, "connect you", or the phone number fails. Text after the call isn't checked for this. It fails all four recorded doubles and more paraphrases. It also fails a single pre-tool line that the answer doesn't repeat word for word. Scope: within the issue
  - not chosen: (c) The exact text counted. The check fails when `ESCALATION_MESSAGE` or the phone number appears twice in the turn. It's simple, but it fails none of the recorded doubles, so AC 4's "seen failing" needs a hand-built transcript. Scope: within the issue
